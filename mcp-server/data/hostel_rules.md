# 🏢 Nimbus Labs Residential Life: Hostel Rules & Regulations

**Term:** Academic Year 2026–2027  
**Applies To:** North & West Residence Halls  

---

## 1. Curfew & Gate Timings
- Main residence gates close strictly at **22:30 (10:30 PM)** daily.
- Late entry between 22:30 and 23:30 requires biometric verification and logging at the Warden's Desk.
- Gate access after 23:30 is permitted only with prior approved leave pass submitted through the Student Portal 12 hours in advance.

## 2. Visitor Regulations
- Non-resident visitors are permitted in common lounges between **09:00 and 20:00**.
- No external visitors or students from other hostel wings are allowed inside individual resident rooms after 20:00.
- Overnight guests are strictly prohibited without written authorization from the Dean of Student Affairs.

## 3. Quiet Hours & Study Periods
- Daily Quiet Hours are observed from **23:00 to 06:00**.
- During Examination Weeks (Midterms & Finals), 24-hour quiet period is maintained across all corridors and common study zones.

## 4. Prohibited Items & Safety
- High-wattage appliances (>1000W) including electric induction stoves, space heaters, and immersion coils are not allowed.
- Tampering with smoke detectors or fire alarms incurs an immediate administrative fine of $150 and disciplinary review.
- Smoking, vaping, and possession of alcoholic beverages are strictly forbidden on all university residential premises.
